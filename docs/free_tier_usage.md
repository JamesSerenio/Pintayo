# Pilot usage and costs

Design targets a small pilot, not unlimited free operation. Review [Supabase pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing) and [billing documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/billing-on-supabase) for the current project allowances before rollout. The pricing pages were consulted on 29 October 2026; this project deliberately avoids hard-coding allowance numbers that may change. Track database size, Storage size, egress, Realtime concurrent connections/message fan-out, Auth use and Edge Function invocations in the dashboard.

Each coordinate is one Postgres RPC, not an Edge Function call. Recipient rows are selected from explicit friend grants and snapshotted circle recipients. One fix to ten recipients can produce ten realtime deliveries; presence and typing contribute additional messages. Movement throttling, OS distance filtering, small payloads and only the current fix reduce usage. The active map also reconciles a bounded inbox every 30 seconds to clear revocations and update freshness; stop/leave subscriptions outside their visible scope. Message history pages contain 40 messages; catch-up batches contain 200. General lists currently cap at 100, meetups at 50.

Images are compressed JPEGs with EXIF removed. Storage limits are 2 MB for avatars and 5 MB for attachments. Replacing an avatar removes its old saved object when possible. Add retention and orphan cleanup to your operating policy before expansion; durable messages/photos consume storage until you deliberately remove them. Downloading photos and map tiles consumes bandwidth on both service and device sides.

Push dispatch is batched from a durable queue. The example once-per-minute schedule invokes a worker even when no event exists; adjust it for pilot latency and quota needs. Empty queues avoid requesting an FCM access token. No push notification is emitted for each coordinate. Monitor failed dispatch attempts instead of increasing retries indefinitely.

Public OpenStreetMap standard tiles are suitable only when your usage complies with the [tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/): identify the app, keep attribution, honor caching, and never bulk-download/prefetch. They are donated infrastructure with no unlimited hosting commitment or service-level guarantee. Configure a suitable commercial/self-hosted OSM-based provider when pilot traffic requires it; provider pricing is separate from Supabase.

App distribution may require separate Apple Developer Program and Google Play registration fees. Review [Apple membership](https://developer.apple.com/programs/) and [Google Play registration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435) for your country/account; no fixed fee is assumed here. SMTP/email delivery, domains, signing memberships, tile hosting and future road routing can have separate costs. FCM is the only Firebase product integrated; do not add other Firebase products merely to support notifications.
